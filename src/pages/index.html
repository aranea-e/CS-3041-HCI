Spellbook Creator Task

You will attempt to replicate a given spellbook. Your interaction time and usage of keyboard or search bar options will be recorded locally for usability metrics.

  • Click on a spell slot to choose a spell for that slot
  • Click on an option to add it to that slot
  • A search bar is provided on the spell list to narrow down options
  • You may also use the keyboard to navigate the UI
  • When the spellbook matches the one you are trying to recreate, your data will be recorded and you will be redirected
